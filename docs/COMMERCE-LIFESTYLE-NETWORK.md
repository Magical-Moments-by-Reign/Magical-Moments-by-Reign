## 1. Purpose & Positioning

The intelligent layer that turns Magical Moments into a **Luxury Life Operating System.** It does
not replace airlines, hotels, restaurants, ticket sellers, or stores — it sits **above** them,
understands the member, and assembles complete experiences through **authorized** partners and
integrations. The concierge is the single front door; commerce and lifestyle are how it *acts* on
a member's dreams, honestly.

This layer is **cross-cutting** — it enriches every Estate (Travel, Home, Celebrations, Family,
Relationship, Dream…) rather than being a separate destination.

---

## 2. Philosophy

We are not building pages — we are building **experiences.** We are not organizing tasks — we are
**organizing dreams**, then helping members bring them to life. The concierge reframes
transactions as memories:
- Not "what restaurant?" but **"what kind of memory are we creating tonight?"**
- Not "here are cheaper options" but **"here's what fits you, and why."**

Every recommendation is honest, member-shaped, and explained. Nothing is advertised that isn't
real and available; nothing is charged without authorization.

## 3. Member Lifestyle Profiles

Every member chooses how Journey recommends — globally and **per Estate**:
- **Luxury First** · **Best Value** · **Budget Conscious** · **Smart Saver** · **Custom Per
  Estate.**

Example: *Travel = Luxury · Home = Best Value · Shopping = Smart Saver · Business = Premium.*

Stored on the member's concierge preferences (Step 4 personalization). Every recommendation adapts
to the active profile; the member can change it anytime, and the concierge explains how the profile
shaped a suggestion.

---

## 4. Recommendation Model (honest, explained, member-shaped)

Every recommendation:
- **Adapts to the lifestyle profile** and the member's stated criteria (date, budget, party size,
  location, accessibility, preferences).
- **Explains WHY it appears** — "closest to your venue, top-rated for families, within your
  budget," never an unexplained ranking.
- **Never recommends lower quality solely because it's cheaper** — value ≠ cheapest; safety,
  convenience, and preferences come first.
- **Prioritizes safety and convenience** (e.g. hotel neighborhood safety over a marginally lower
  price).
- **Discloses when Magical Moments may earn** from a booking/purchase (affiliate/commerce), using
  the same sponsored-placement labeling rule as the Partner Ecosystem (§14 there). No hidden
  pay-to-rank.
- **Uses real, current data** from authorized providers — prices, availability, ratings, and
  reviews are never fabricated (constitution).

## 18. Concierge Orchestration

The concierge is how this layer is experienced — it **assembles** the complete experience:
- Understands the moment and the member's lifestyle profile, then proposes options with reasons.
- Chains steps across categories (dinner → flowers → transportation → babysitter) as one plan, using
  the Planning Engine.
- **Verifies availability before offering** (never advertises the unavailable).
- **Confirms before any consequential action** — booking, reserving, purchasing, sharing payment
  info, contacting a provider (Concierge Architecture §11; Partner Ecosystem §23).
- **Discloses** deposits, cancellation terms, who processes payment, and any Magical commission,
  every time.
- Adapts tone to context (a proposal vs. a business dinner vs. a memorial gathering).

## 19. Integration Boundaries (authorized only; honest until connected)

| Capability | Rule |
|------------|------|
| Travel inventory (flights/hotels/rail/cars) | Only via authorized provider integrations; live data or honest "not connected yet" |
| Reservations (restaurants/experiences) | Only via supported reservation partners; never fake a booking |
| Entertainment tickets | Only through authorized providers (Ticketmaster/AXS/…); **never bypass their systems** |
| Theme-park tickets | Official channels only; no unofficial resale |
| Discounts/benefits | Real & official; verification through the provider's official process |
| Cashback/rebates/affiliate | Real programs only; commission disclosed |
| Commerce comparisons | Real retailer data; no invented prices/stock |

**Principle:** until an integration is connected and tested, the feature is **honestly gated** ("this
isn't connected yet — you can still plan, and I'll notify you when it's available"). No mock inventory
is presented as real.

## 20. Payments, Disclosure & Authorization

- **Square remains the intended payment seam** where Magical processes payment; otherwise the member
  pays the provider directly.
- **Every time money is involved**, disclose before authorization: amount · deposits · cancellation
  policy · payment requirements · **who processes the payment** (Magical vs. the provider) · any
  Magical commission/fee.
- **Nothing is charged without the member's explicit authorization.** No pre-authorized surprises.
- Payment is **not presented as active until connected and tested** (consistent with Partner
  Ecosystem §22).

## 21. Trust & Safety (commerce/lifestyle specifics)

This layer must **never:** advertise an unavailable service · invent availability, prices, discounts,
rebates, or reviews · fabricate eligibility for a benefit · imply a partnership or integration that
doesn't exist · bypass a provider's official system · recommend lower quality solely because it's
cheaper · hide when Magical earns a commission · charge without authorization · present estimated
prices as final · sacrifice safety/convenience for price.

It must **always:** explain WHY a recommendation appears · disclose commissions and who processes
payment · verify availability before offering · keep regulated topics (insurance, estate/financial,
visa) educational and route to official/licensed sources · respect the member's lifestyle profile and
privacy.

---

## 22. Honest Empty & Failure States

- *No availability for the date/area:* "I couldn't find availability for that night — here are a few
  elegant alternatives," never a fabricated slot.
- *Integration not connected:* "Live booking for this isn't connected yet — you can plan now and I'll
  notify you when it opens."
- *No qualifying discount / can't verify eligibility:* "I can't confirm a discount here — here's the
  official place to check your eligibility."
- *Provider system down:* honest status + save-and-retry.
- *Price/stock changed:* re-fetch and show the real current value; never stale-as-current.

**Never** fabricate a fallback booking, price, or partner to fill a gap.
