# Development Governance & Project Philosophy

Magical Moments by Reign is developed using the same governance model as
Project Legacy. This is not a traditional software project — it is a
vision-driven platform. **Every feature, workflow, automation, page,
integration, pricing model, AI interaction, and customer experience must be
documented before development begins.** Nothing is final until reviewed and
approved by the Founder.

---

## Founder Authority

**Tabitha Coetha Turner** is the Founder, CEO, and Chief Visionary of Magical
Moments by Reign. She has final approval over:

Platform Architecture · User Experience · Customer Journeys · AI Behavior ·
Business Rules · Pricing · Features · Branding · Visual Design · Integrations ·
Automations · Development Priorities · Product Roadmap · Public Releases.

Development recommendations are encouraged. **Implementation decisions belong to
the Founder.**

---

## Development Process

Every major feature follows this process:

1. Vision Discussion
2. Master Design Bible Documentation
3. Workflow Design
4. Founder Review
5. Founder Approval
6. Development
7. Internal Testing
8. Founder Acceptance Testing
9. Revisions (if required)
10. Production Release

Development should never skip Founder approval.

---

## Change Management

Claude (Journey AI) may **recommend** better workflows, better user experiences,
technical improvements, security enhancements, performance improvements, and
scalability improvements.

However, recommendations are not final decisions. Every recommendation requires
Founder review before becoming part of the platform.

---

## Project Discipline

- Do not assume.
- Do not redesign completed features without discussion.
- Do not remove functionality without approval.
- Do not change business logic without approval.
- Do not alter pricing without approval.
- Do not change branding without approval.
- If something appears to conflict with the Master Design Bible, **stop and
  request clarification** rather than making assumptions.

---

## Our Shared Goal

We are building a platform intended to serve families for generations. Every
decision should support the long-term vision established by the Founder.

**The Master Design Bible is the single source of truth.** If documentation and
existing code ever conflict, notify the Founder so the correct direction can be
confirmed before continuing.

---

## Final Statement

The goal is not simply to build software. The goal is to faithfully bring the
Founder's vision to life. Every line of code should move Magical Moments by
Reign closer to becoming the world's premier AI-powered Life Journey Platform.

**No feature is considered complete until the Founder has reviewed it, approved
it, and accepted it for release.**
