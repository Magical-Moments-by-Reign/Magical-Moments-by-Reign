# Master Design Bible — Baby Journey
## Pregnancy Experience · Digital Pregnancy Record · AI Baby Assistant

**Status:** Documented — pending Founder approval to build.
**External services (later phases):** SMS + push notifications, AI text
generation for weekly updates/summaries, speech-to-text (voice journal),
secure media storage. Subordinate to Book I.

The Baby Journey is not a baby website — it's an **AI-powered pregnancy
companion** from the positive test through the child's milestones. It educates,
organizes, celebrates, preserves memories, and reduces stress. It never
replaces doctors or medical advice.

---

## Welcome & setup

Ask: Are you expecting? · Already delivered? · Adopting? · Creating as a
surprise gift? If expecting: estimated due date, doctor/hospital (optional),
baby nickname, baby name (if known), expected gender (or surprise), multiples?,
first child?

## 40-week timeline

Auto-create a weekly journey (Week 4 → Week 40); each week unlocks
automatically. **Weekly update** includes: baby's approx size (e.g. "about the
size of an avocado"), development & organs, weight/length, milestones, prep
tips, nutrition & hydration reminders, questions to ask the doctor, self-care,
an encouraging message for Mom, and a due-date countdown. Optional illustration
/ 3D visualization.

**Delivery options (parent's choice):** Email · SMS · Push · In-App · any
combination.

## Mom's Journey

Track separately: positive test, first appointment, first ultrasound,
heartbeat, morning sickness, cravings, bump photos, maternity shoot, baby
shower, hospital bag, birth plan, final countdown.

## Appointments

Schedule/track: doctor visits, ultrasounds, bloodwork, specialists,
vaccinations, hospital tours, prenatal classes. Each: date, time, provider,
location, purpose, notes, questions asked/answers, files, photos/videos,
follow-up, reminders, next appointment. Every appointment becomes a milestone.

## Ultrasound Memory Vault

Uploads: 2D/3D/4D photos, videos, heartbeat recordings, printed scans, doctor
summaries — auto-organized by pregnancy week and appointment date.

## Secure hospital & provider uploads

Parents generate a **one-time, secure, expiring upload link** for approved
providers (hospital, OB/GYN, ultrasound/fertility clinic, midwife, birth
photographer) to upload photos, videos, birth paperwork, footprints, weight
card, hospital keepsakes — **without access to the full Journey.** Link expires
after use or on a chosen date. (Reuses the existing secure-upload pattern.)

## Journal & Digital Pregnancy Record

A private record that grows across pregnancy and becomes the child's opening
chapter. After each appointment the AI asks "How did today's appointment go?"
Parents answer by typing, voice note, video, or uploads. Prompts: how are you
feeling, what did the doctor say, new milestone, changes, next steps, concerns.
Dad and grandparents may add messages/letters.

## Baby bump timeline

Weekly bump photos auto-arranged into a growing timeline with side-by-side
comparisons and a generated pregnancy slideshow.

## Registry (see Gifts & Registries standard)

Offer: Registry · Cash Gifts · Diaper Fund · College Fund · Meal Train ·
Wishlist. (Never assumed; uses the Gifts & Registries engine.)

## Checklists & AI Baby Assistant

Interactive checklists with progress: Nursery, Hospital Bag, Baby Clothes, Car
Seat, Bassinet, Insurance, Pediatrician, Birth Plan, and a full Smart Baby
Checklist. **Baby Essentials Guide** recommends items by stage (mark purchased /
save / add to registry / add to shopping list / share / ask AI why it helps).
**Hospital Bag Assistant** begins wk 32–34 ("It's almost time to pack…").
**Labor Countdown** from wk 36 (car seat, pediatrician, birth plan, route to
hospital). **Personalized shopping** by season/climate/twins/feeding/living/
travel/budget — always optional. **Postpartum reminders** (first pediatrician
visit, rest, first smile, monthly photos). **Milestone reminders** (first
smile/laugh/roll/sit/tooth/crawl/steps/word/first birthday).

**AI companion tone:** supportive, organized, encouraging — never intrusive.
Gentle check-ins between appointments; suggests bump photos, letters to baby,
question lists before ultrasounds.

## Memory Book

Generate a designed Pregnancy Memory Book (weekly updates, ultrasounds, bump
photos, journal, appointment summaries, letters, voice-note QR codes,
milestones, countdown, birth story). Export: digital flipbook, printable PDF;
future: hardcover, keepsake box.

## After delivery → the family story

Auto-transition into the Newborn Journey (monthly milestones, firsts, first
birthday). The pregnancy chapter is **never archived** — years later the child
can experience the first test, ultrasounds, heartbeat, letters, videos, birth,
coming home, and growing up.

---

## Recommended build phasing

- **Phase A:** setup + 40-week timeline (static weekly content), Mom's Journey,
  appointments, ultrasound vault, journal (text), bump timeline, checklists,
  registry (existing engine). No external AI needed.
- **Phase B:** secure provider upload links (existing pattern), Memory Book
  export (PDF).
- **Phase C:** AI companion updates/summaries + voice journal (needs AI +
  speech-to-text), multi-channel reminders (needs SMS/push).

**Guardrail:** informational only, never medical advice; AI summaries always
reviewed by the parent; provider links never expose the full Journey.
