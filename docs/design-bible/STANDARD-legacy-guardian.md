# Master Design Bible — Account Continuity & Transfer
## Legacy Guardian™

**Status:** Documented — pending Founder approval to build.
**Needs:** multi-user accounts/auth, a secure verification workflow, and
(for transfer) ownership-reassignment across the Family Vault + Journeys.
Subordinate to Book I. (™ shown per the Founder's canonical text; use in UI
only once the trademark process is complete.)

Every account may designate one or more trusted **Legacy Guardians** to help
ensure a family's memories and important information remain protected if the
owner becomes unable to manage the account.

---

## Setup

Encourage adding at least one trusted person at setup (spouse, adult child,
parent, sibling, attorney, trusted friend). Store: name, email, phone,
relationship, optional mailing address. The owner may change or remove a
guardian at any time. *(Groundwork exists today: Family Vault → Trusted
Contacts with access levels.)*

## When a guardian may act

Being listed grants **no access** by itself. Special permissions unlock only
after a defined account-continuity process — e.g. death of the owner,
long-term hospitalization, extended incapacity, or other situations the owner
defines.

## Continuity options

- **Monthly/Annual membership:** request Journey Pause, membership cancellation,
  billing hold (if eligible), preserve account and memories.
- **Lifetime membership:** request ownership transfer → become the new account
  administrator after verification → continue preserving the family's Journeys.

## Verification (never automatic)

Account changes go through a secure review: identity verification, confirmation
using information already on the account, verification via the designated
guardian, and additional documentation when appropriate. Goal: prevent
unauthorized access while helping families in hard times.

## Ownership transfer (eligible Lifetime)

After verification + approval: the Family Vault, Journeys, galleries, and
documents transfer intact; the Family Timeline continues; the guardian becomes
the new owner. Nothing is lost — the family's story continues.

---

## Founder philosophy

Preserve family legacies across generations — protect memories today and ensure
they can be responsibly cared for tomorrow. The owner stays in control while
able; when life changes, the platform helps families preserve what matters with
compassion, security, and respect.

## Recommended build phasing

- **Phase A (now-ish):** capture Legacy Guardians in the Family Vault (extend
  Trusted Contacts with a "Legacy Guardian" flag + relationship/contact) — data
  only, no special access.
- **Phase B:** the secure continuity-request + verification workflow (needs
  auth + a reviewed process; likely human-in-the-loop).
- **Phase C:** ownership transfer tooling for Lifetime accounts (reassign
  Family + Journeys + documents to the new owner) — gated behind verified
  approval.

**Guardrail:** no automatic access or transfer ever; every continuity action is
verified and, where appropriate, human-reviewed.
