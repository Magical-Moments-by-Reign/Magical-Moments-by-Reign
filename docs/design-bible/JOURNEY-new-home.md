# Master Design Bible — New Home Journey
## The Complete Homeowner Experience

**Status:** Documented — pending Founder approval to build.
**External services (later phases):** lender/vendor directory data, secure
document storage, AI reminders. Subordinate to Book I.

Guide customers from the dream of owning a home through years of successful
homeownership — removing uncertainty, reducing stress, organizing documents,
and helping families confidently care for their home.

## Step 1 — Where are you?

Looking to Buy · Building a Home · Buying New Construction · Buying Existing
Home · Selling Current Home · Renting.

## Buying roadmap (AI-guided)

Check credit → budget → savings goal → mortgage pre-approval → find realtor →
find lender → house hunting → make offer → inspection → appraisal → final
walkthrough → closing day → move-in.

## Mortgage Center (plain-language education)

Fixed vs adjustable, FHA, VA, USDA, conventional, jumbo, down-payment
assistance, PMI, closing costs, refinancing, interest rates.

## Lender Directory

Browse mortgage companies, banks, credit unions, local lenders, VA/FHA
specialists — each with contact, reviews, website, hours, appointment request.
*(Needs vendor/lender data — gated seam.)*

## Checklists

- **Home Buyer:** pre-approval letter, pay stubs, W-2s, tax returns, bank
  statements, photo ID, proof of insurance, earnest money, utility transfer,
  address change, home warranty.
- **Closing Day:** photo ID, certified funds, insurance binder, appointment,
  final walkthrough, questions for the closing attorney, celebrate.
- **Move-In:** room-by-room + essentials (smoke detectors, fire extinguisher,
  first aid, tools, filters, light bulbs, supplies…).

## Utility Center

By property address, build a personalized checklist: electric, gas, water,
sewer, trash/recycling, internet, cable/streaming, phone, security, HOA. Save
account numbers, service numbers, transfer dates; mark activated; upload
confirmations; save billing info.

## Home Information

Purchase price, closing date, mortgage company, loan number, interest rate,
escrow, property taxes, insurance, HOA, survey, inspection reports, builder
warranty, appliance manuals, paint colors, flooring, roof warranty. *(Overlaps
the Family Vault Home Center — shared store.)*

## Home Services directory

Electricians, plumbers, HVAC, roofers, landscapers, cleaners, painters, pest

## Build-a-Home Experience (blueprint → front-door key)

A dedicated **"Build a Home"** pathway inside the New Home Journey that guides a
customer from first idea and floor plan through construction, final inspection,
closing, and move-in. The finished construction Journey becomes the **permanent
history of the home.**

### Starting the journey (intake → personalized roadmap)

Ask: own land already? · builder selected? · architectural plans? · financing /
construction-loan approval? · target budget? · city & state? · desired
completion date? — then generate a personalized roadmap from the answers.

### Floor Plan Center

Upload floor plans, architectural drawings, blueprints, site plans, surveys,
elevations, inspiration photos, room sketches, builder specifications, finish
schedules (PDF, image, common docs). Customers can organize by **version**,
compare old vs new, add per-room notes, approve/reject revisions, share selected
files with builders/lenders/designers/family, and download/print approved plans.
**Earlier plan versions are never overwritten.** *(Needs secure storage.)*

### Room-by-Room Design

Sections: Exterior · Kitchen · Living Room · Primary Bedroom · Bathrooms ·
Children's Rooms · Office · Laundry · Garage · Patio · Landscaping · Custom
Rooms. Each room saves: paint colors, flooring, cabinets, countertops, fixtures,
appliances, lighting, furniture ideas, product links, pricing, photos, and AI
design previews. Preview color/finish changes before purchasing. *(AI previews =
gated seam.)*

### Build Team

Store & manage: Builder · Architect · Engineer · Interior Designer · Lender ·
Closing Attorney · Realtor · Inspector · Contractors · Vendors · Utility
Providers. Each professional receives **only homeowner-approved access.** Secure
messages, email, file sharing, appointments, video calls. *(Needs auth +
messaging/video seams.)*

### Construction Timeline (28 stages)

Budget & financing → Land search/purchase → Survey & soil testing → Floor-plan
approval → Permits → Site prep → Foundation → Framing → Roofing → Windows &
exterior doors → Plumbing rough-in → Electrical rough-in → HVAC → Insulation →
Drywall → Cabinets & countertops → Flooring → Painting → Fixtures & appliances →
Exterior finishes → Landscaping → Inspections → Punch list → Final walkthrough →
Certificate of occupancy → Closing → Move-in → Housewarming.

Each stage supports: start/completion dates · photos & videos · documents ·
costs · payments · notes · approvals · delays · inspection results · assigned
contacts · next steps.

### Budget & Payment Tracker

Original construction budget · land cost · builder contract · allowances ·
change orders · deposits · draw payments · upgrades · credits · closing costs ·
amount paid · amount remaining. Warn when selections/change orders may push over
budget. **Always require the customer to confirm costs with their builder or
lender.**

### Change-Order Center

Each change: description · reason · requested date · estimated cost · schedule
impact · builder response · customer approval · signed document · final amount.
**No change order is marked approved without clear customer confirmation.**

### Construction Loan & Mortgage Center

Plain-language education on construction loans, construction-to-permanent loans,
draw schedules, down payments, interest during construction, appraisals,
inspections, conversion to permanent financing, closing costs. Save lenders,
request appointments, upload documents, track loan milestones. **Organizational
& educational assistance only — not mortgage, legal, engineering, or
construction advice.**

### Inspections & Punch List

Inspection checklists per major stage; upload reports, photograph concerns,
assign items to the builder, set due dates, track open/completed repairs, approve
work, export the punch list as PDF.

### Closing Preparation

Final walkthrough · certificate of occupancy · homeowners insurance · utility
activation · closing disclosure · ID · required funds · warranty documents ·
keys & access codes · appliance manuals · builder contacts · final loan
documents. Reminders before closing + a printable closing checklist.

### Move-In → Home Center

After closing, transition into the Home Center, preserving: final floor plans,
warranties, paint colors, flooring details, appliance info, contractor contacts,
maintenance schedules, insurance inventory, build photos/videos, closing
memories, housewarming registry — the permanent history of the home.

### The final experience

Years later the customer can look back on: the original dream · the empty land ·
every floor-plan version · foundation day · framing · room selections ·
construction progress · final walkthrough · closing day · first night at home.

## Recommended build phasing

- **Phase A (self-contained, buildable now):** New Home roadmap + checklists
  (buyer/closing/move-in) + Utility Center + Home Information + Home Inventory +
  Housewarming (reuses experiences, gifts, guestbook). **Build-a-Home first
  slice: the intake → personalized roadmap + the informational 28-stage
  construction timeline** (what each stage captures) — self-contained, like the
  Wedding Journey planner's first slice.
- **Phase B:** Build-a-Home data centers that need persistence + storage —
  Floor Plan Center (versioned uploads), Room-by-Room Design, Budget & Payment
  Tracker, Change-Order Center, Inspections/Punch List. *(Needs auth + secure
  storage + Family Vault Home Center.)*
- **Phase C:** Build Team collaboration (approved-access messaging/email/video),
  Lender & Home-Services directories + appointment requests, AI design previews
  & AI Home Assistant reminders. *(Needs vendor data / messaging / video / AI
  seams.)*

**Sequencing note:** the full Build-a-Home data centers (Phases B–C) sit in a
later phase of the approved Foundation Build Order (we are on Phase 1 — Family
Vault). Phase A's informational Build-a-Home slice is shippable now without
disturbing that order.

**Guardrail:** educational only (not financial/legal/engineering/construction
advice); directories/AI gated until real data/providers exist; documents stored
per Family Vault permissions; plan versions never overwritten; change orders and
costs always confirmed by the customer with their builder/lender.
