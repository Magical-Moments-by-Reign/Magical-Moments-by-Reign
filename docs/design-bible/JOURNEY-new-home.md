# Master Design Bible — New Home Journey
## The Complete Homeowner Experience

**Status:** Documented — pending Founder approval to build.
**External services (later phases):** lender/vendor directory data, secure
document storage, AI reminders. Subordinate to Book I.

Guide customers from the dream of owning a home through years of successful
homeownership — removing uncertainty, reducing stress, organizing documents,
and helping families confidently care for their home.

---

## Step 1 — Where are you?

Looking to Buy · Building a Home · Buying New Construction · Buying Existing
Home · Selling Current Home · Renting.

## Buying roadmap (AI-guided)

Check credit → budget → savings goal → mortgage pre-approval → find realtor →
find lender → house hunting → make offer → inspection → appraisal → final
walkthrough → closing day → move-in.

## Mortgage Center (plain-language education)

Fixed vs adjustable, FHA, VA, USDA, conventional, jumbo, down-payment
assistance, PMI, closing costs, refinancing, interest rates.

## Lender Directory

Browse mortgage companies, banks, credit unions, local lenders, VA/FHA
specialists — each with contact, reviews, website, hours, appointment request.
*(Needs vendor/lender data — gated seam.)*

## Checklists

- **Home Buyer:** pre-approval letter, pay stubs, W-2s, tax returns, bank
  statements, photo ID, proof of insurance, earnest money, utility transfer,
  address change, home warranty.
- **Closing Day:** photo ID, certified funds, insurance binder, appointment,
  final walkthrough, questions for the closing attorney, celebrate.
- **Move-In:** room-by-room + essentials (smoke detectors, fire extinguisher,
  first aid, tools, filters, light bulbs, supplies…).

## Utility Center

By property address, build a personalized checklist: electric, gas, water,
sewer, trash/recycling, internet, cable/streaming, phone, security, HOA. Save
account numbers, service numbers, transfer dates; mark activated; upload
confirmations; save billing info.

## Home Information

Purchase price, closing date, mortgage company, loan number, interest rate,
escrow, property taxes, insurance, HOA, survey, inspection reports, builder
warranty, appliance manuals, paint colors, flooring, roof warranty. *(Overlaps
the Family Vault Home Center — shared store.)*

## Home Services directory

Electricians, plumbers, HVAC, roofers, landscapers, cleaners, painters, pest
control, handyman, interior designers, movers. *(Needs vendor data.)*

## AI Home Assistant

"Replace your HVAC filter this month." · "Six months since pest control." ·
"Test your smoke detectors." · "Schedule annual HVAC maintenance?" · "Insurance
renews next month."

## Home Inventory

Room-by-room: photos, videos, receipts, serial numbers, purchase dates,
estimated values, warranty, insurance notes — exportable for insurance claims.

## Housewarming

Website, guest list, RSVP, registry, cash gifts (existing Gifts engine), food
planning, photo gallery, guestbook, virtual tour.

---

## Recommended build phasing

- **Phase A:** roadmap + checklists (buyer/closing/move-in) + Utility Center +
  Home Information + Home Inventory + Housewarming (reuses experiences, gifts,
  guestbook). Self-contained.
- **Phase B:** Home Center reminders (AI) + Family Vault integration.
- **Phase C:** Lender & Home-Services directories + appointment requests (needs
  vendor data / APIs).

**Guardrail:** educational only (not financial/legal advice); directories gated
until real data exists; documents stored per Family Vault permissions.
