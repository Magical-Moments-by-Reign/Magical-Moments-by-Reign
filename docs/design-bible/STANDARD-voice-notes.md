# Master Design Bible — Global Feature
## Voice Notes & AI Transcription — "Speak Instead"

**Status:** Documented — pending Founder approval to build.
**External services required:** speech-to-text transcription + AI text cleanup;
audio storage. Subordinate to Book I. Available everywhere a customer can type.

Let customers preserve memories naturally without stopping to type.

---

## Voice to memory

Tap 🎙 **Speak** and talk naturally ("My daughter loved her birthday party
today…", "We finally picked our wedding venue…"). The platform converts speech
into organized journal entries.

## AI Smart Notes

After transcription, the AI produces clean formatting, punctuation, grammar,
paragraphs, headings, dates, timeline placement, and searchable keywords. The
original recording is preserved unless the customer deletes it.

**Save both:** voice recording, written transcript, or both — so families can
later hear the original voice while reading the memory.

## Smart tagging

Auto-recognized, searchable tags: doctor, wedding planner, venue, birthday,
vacation, baby, graduation, home, anniversary, school, family members, pets,
locations, products, appointments.

## Doctor appointments

After a visit the AI asks "Would you like to tell me about today's visit?" and
produces an appointment summary, doctor notes, growth, questions asked,
recommendations, follow-up tasks, next appointment, and a journal entry.

## Voice reminders & voice letters

- **Reminders by voice:** "Remind me to buy diapers tomorrow." → task created.
- **Voice letters:** to a baby, future spouse, graduating child, parent,
  grandchildren, or oneself — unlockable immediately or on a birthday,
  graduation, wedding, 18th birthday, retirement, or a custom date.

## Meeting transcripts

After approved video calls, generate a summary, action items, decisions, tasks,
and deadlines — always reviewed by the owner before saving.

## Global availability

Wedding, Proposal, Baby, Birthday, Graduation, Vacation, New Home, Memorial,
Military, Retirement, Family Vault, Message Center, vendor meetings, journal
entries — everywhere you can type, you can speak.

---

## Recommended build phasing

- **Phase A:** record + store voice notes attached to journals/tributes/
  messages (no transcription) — playable keepsakes. No external AI needed.
- **Phase B:** transcription + AI Smart Notes + smart tagging (needs
  speech-to-text + AI).
- **Phase C:** voice reminders, voice letters (scheduled unlock), meeting
  transcripts.

**Guardrail:** recordings kept private to the owner's chosen scope; AI
transcripts/summaries always reviewed before being relied upon; nothing
recorded secretly.
