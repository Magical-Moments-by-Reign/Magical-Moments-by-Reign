# Master Design Bible — Book III
## The Family Vault — "The Heart of Every Family."

**Status:** Documented — pending Founder approval to build.
**External services (later):** encryption-at-rest for sensitive documents,
secure file storage, AI reminders, wallet passes (future). Subordinate to Book I.

While **Journeys preserve memories, the Family Vault preserves life.** It is the
secure command center a family uses to protect, organize, and access everything
they need — encrypted, permission-based, on any device. It belongs to the
**Family Account** and supports every Journey.

## Family member profiles

Each member (Mom, Dad, Child, Grandparent, Guardian, Pet) has a secure profile:
full/preferred name, birthday, blood type (optional), allergies, medications,
doctors, insurance, emergency contacts, favorite things, notes, and Journey
connections.

## Emergency Center & Emergency Mode

Quick-access emergency profile: insurance cards, medical conditions, allergies,
medications, doctor/dentist/hospital, emergency contacts, authorized pick-up
list, blood type (optional), medical/travel consent + authorization forms.

**Emergency Mode** (one touch, owner chooses what shows): Call Mom / Call Dad /
Call 911 / Pediatrician / Preferred Hospital / Current Medications / Allergies /
Insurance Card / Emergency Authorization / Home Address / Directions to
Hospital.

**Emergency Access Card:** contains only what the owner chooses; printable,
PDF, QR-protected, future Apple/Google Wallet. Scanning the QR shows **only**
the approved emergency profile — no private family info is exposed.

## Document Library

Birth certificates, SSNs, passports, driver licenses, marriage/divorce docs,
school & report cards, immunization records, insurance policies, medical
records, tax docs, wills, trusts, home closing/mortgage, vehicle titles, pet
records, military, college, employment, legal, and custom categories.

## School Command Center (per child)

School info, teacher/principal/nurse contacts, bus route, student ID, class
schedule, supply lists, report cards, awards, sports, lunch account, permission
slips, emergency contacts, pickup authorization, calendar, sports physical,
medical/immunization forms, school photos. Generates a printable Emergency
Information Sheet.

## Home · Vehicle · Pet centers

- **Home:** property info, builder, warranty, paint colors, flooring, appliance
  manuals, utilities, HOA, insurance, security codes, smart-home devices,
  maintenance schedule, roof/HVAC service, filter changes, improvement records.
- **Vehicle:** VIN, insurance, registration, loan, oil changes, maintenance,
  warranty, tire rotation, service records.
- **Pet:** vet, vaccinations, microchip, medications, insurance, foods,
  emergency contact, medical records.

## Family Calendar

One intelligent calendar combining doctor appointments, school events,
birthdays, sports, trips, vacations, anniversaries, home/vehicle/pet
maintenance, and all Journeys.

## AI Family Assistant

e.g. "Your child's sports physical expires next month." · "Your passport
expires in 90 days." · "Homeowner's insurance renews next week." · "HVAC filter
due." · "School starts in two weeks." · "Vehicle registration expires soon."
Delivered via email, text, push, and dashboard.

## Family Notebook / Family Safe

Private notes and keepsakes: favorite restaurants, vacation ideas, holiday
traditions, recipes, gift ideas, emergency plans, trusted babysitters,
neighborhood info, important phone numbers, letters, voice messages, home
inventory, warranty info, safe-deposit-box info, trusted contacts, passwords
(future secure vault).

## Permissions (granular)

Per user, owner-granted only. Examples: Mom/Dad = full; Grandparents = medical
emergency only; Babysitter = emergency contacts only; Adult child = personal
documents only; Attorney = legal folder only. No user can access anything
outside granted permissions.

## Document reminders

AI monitors expirations: passport (90 days), insurance updates, sports physical,
license renewal, school registration, vaccination records — via email/text/
push/dashboard.

## The promise

**Memories belong in Journeys. Life belongs in the Family Vault.** Together they
create one secure, organized, beautiful home for every family's story. As new
Journeys are created, they connect back to the Vault — the foundation every
Journey is built on.

## Recommended build phasing

- **Phase A:** Family profiles + Document Library + per-child School Center +
  Emergency Center (data + printable sheet). Needs the Family Account model +
  secure storage.
- **Phase B:** Home/Vehicle/Pet centers, Family Calendar aggregation, document
  expiration reminders.
- **Phase C:** Emergency Mode + QR Emergency Access Card, AI Family Assistant,
  wallet passes.

**Guardrail:** encryption for sensitive documents, strict permission checks on
every read, QR emergency view exposes only the approved subset, and the platform
never surfaces documents beyond a user's granted scope.
