# Master Design Bible — Brand Language, Account & Commerce Vision

**Status:** Founder Approved (canonical product direction). Subordinate to the
[Constitution](./CONSTITUTION.md) and Book I. Magical Moments is **not** a
generic event-website builder — it is a complete life-event **planning,
celebration, collaboration, and memory-preservation** platform, and every screen
reinforces the Magical Moments brand.

---

## Brand language (use consistently)
- **Magical Moments** · **Magical AI** · **Magical Moments Library** ·
  **Magical Tracker™** · **Magical Invitations** · **Magical Galleries** ·
  **Magical Pay™** (future).

**Do NOT** name features "Journey Tracker," "Legacy Library," "Legacy Timeline,"
or similar — **Project Legacy is a separate company and product.** *(Applied:
customer-facing "Legacy/Family Legacy Timeline" copy is renamed to **Magical
Moments Library.**)*

**Reconciliation — these Founder-authored names stay** (they are specific
trademarked offerings, not the avoided generic terms): **Lifetime Legacy**
(Collection), **Legacy Guardian™**, **Legacy Transfer™**, and **Legacy Family**
(loyalty pricing). The word "legacy" may still appear in prose (e.g., "digital
legacy") — the rule targets the *feature names* Timeline/Library/Tracker.

## Magical Tracker™
Every purchase automatically gets a **Magical Tracker** — real-time progress of
the Experience from purchase through completion. Stages include: Purchase
Confirmed · Website Created · AI Activated · Invitations Designed · Invitations
Sent · RSVP Updates · Registry Connected · Guest Messages · Vendor Progress ·
Appointment Reminders · Countdown · Photo Uploads · Video Uploads · Highlight
Video · Memory Album Completed. Customers always know exactly where they are.
*(Stages are known and can render now; live status needs the account/order
state — foundation-dependent.)*

## Magical Moments Library
Every customer has **one** Magical Moments Library storing everything connected
to their account: Experiences · Photos · Videos · Invitations · Guestbooks ·
Registries · Messages · Orders · Purchases · Receipts · AI Conversations ·
Memory Albums · Highlight Videos. **Nothing ever feels lost** — signing in with a
verified email returns the customer exactly where they left off.

## Account synchronization
The **verified email is the permanent account identifier.** Every purchase — now
or years later — auto-connects to that one account and appears in the Library.
**No duplicate accounts, no searching, no missing purchases.**

## Gifting system
Customers can buy an Experience or contribute **as a gift.** Gifted Experiences
attach to the recipient's account once accepted. **Group contributions** let
several people chip in (e.g. Grandmother $100 + Aunt $50 + Friend $25) and the
Experience **unlocks automatically** when the amount is reached.

## Payment roadmap
- **Phase 1:** normal payments (Square) + approved third-party financing
  (**Affirm / Klarna** or similar) for larger purchases.
- **Phase 2:** **Magical Pay™** — in-platform financing for larger purchases,
  built with all applicable legal, financial, and regulatory requirements.

## Purchase philosophy
Customers purchase **Experiences** (Baby Shower, Wedding, Birthday, Graduation,
Prom, Sports, Vacation, Housewarming, Retirement, Celebration of Life…). Each
includes planning tools, collaboration, invitations, galleries, Magical AI, and
memory preservation. Bundles/upgrades may exist later, but the primary purchase
stays simple and centered on individual Experiences.

## Design goal
Every screen feels **magical, organized, elegant, premium, and emotionally
connected** — guiding families through life's biggest celebrations while
preserving those memories forever: eliminate stress, simplify planning,
encourage collaboration, make every milestone unforgettable.
