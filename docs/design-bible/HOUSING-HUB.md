## What it is

Housing Hub is the **complete housing ecosystem** inside Magical Moments.
Customers should never have to search multiple websites to complete a housing
project — Magical Moments becomes their trusted housing guide from beginning to
end. The goal is **not to replace licensed professionals** — it is to organize
the journey, simplify decisions, preserve every document, and help families stay
ahead. Educational & organizational assistance only (not mortgage, legal,
engineering, or construction advice).

## The nine pathways

Each pathway has its own dashboard, timeline, checklist, documents, budget, AI
guidance, and trusted professionals.

1. **Search for Land** — search by location, budget, acreage, school district,
   utilities, zoning, waterfront, neighborhood; save favorites, compare, upload
   surveys/soil reports, track offers, store contracts. When a property becomes
   unavailable, show *"This property is now under contract"* and offer: search
   nearby · expand area · increase budget · pause project.
2. **Purchase an Existing Home** — pre-approval → budget → search → favorites →
   questions to ask → offer → inspection → negotiation → appraisal → insurance →
   closing → move-in. Home Buyer Guide: showing checklists, red flags, estimated
   monthly payment, insurance/taxes/HOA, schools, utilities, comparison tools.
3. **Purchase New Construction** — buy a builder's new home with selections,
   walkthroughs, and closing organized.
4. **Build a Custom Home** *(live — first slice)* — land → blueprint → builder →
   financing → permits → construction → selections → inspections → closing →
   move-in. See [New Home Journey](./JOURNEY-new-home.md) for the full
   Build-a-Home spec (Floor Plan Center, Room-by-Room, Build Team, 28-stage
   timeline, Budget/Change-Order/Loan centers, Inspections, Closing, Move-in).
5. **Renovate a Home** — planning → budget → design → contractors → permits →
   timeline → inspections → completion; track quotes, receipts, materials,
   photos/videos, change orders, before/after galleries.
6. **Sell a Home** — prepare → repairs → cleaning → staging → photography →
   pricing → listing → showings → offers → negotiations → closing → moving.
7. **Lease a Home** — landlords (lease generator, applications, rent tracking,
   deposits, inspections, maintenance, renewals, tenant comms) and tenants
   (lease docs, rent tracking, utilities, insurance, maintenance, move-in/out).
8. **Lease Commercial Property** — commercial leasing organized end to end.
9. **Property Management** — for homeowners & investors: properties, tenants,
   rent, maintenance, repairs, insurance, taxes, vendors, inspections, expenses,
   documents.

## Shared tools (across every path)

- **Home Design Studio** — design every room (flooring, tile, paint, cabinets,
  countertops, lighting, appliances, roof, brick/stone, windows/doors,
  landscaping, pool, outdoor kitchen, smart home); realistic inspiration
  previews; multiple saved design boards. *(AI previews = gated seam.)*
- **Selection Center** — track every material selection: photos, store, brand,
  model #, price, builder allowance, upgrade cost, purchase link, notes,
  approval status, delivery date.
- **Builder Assistant** — recommended questions per meeting; track decisions,
  change orders, approvals; store contracts & warranties; deadlines; printable
  selection sheets.
- **Lease Generator** — customizable leases (residential, commercial,
  month-to-month, fixed-term, room rental, vacation rental, pet/parking
  addenda). *Customers must review and consult a qualified attorney regarding
  local legal requirements before use.*
- **Moving Center** — packing, utilities, internet, mail forwarding, address
  changes, school transfers, moving company, storage, cleaning, move-in list.
- **Home Document Vault** — mortgage, closing docs, insurance, survey, title,
  blueprints, permits, inspections, warranties, HOA docs, tax info, leases,
  receipts, photos/videos. *(Overlaps the Family Vault Home Center — shared
  store, per Vault permissions.)*
- **Housing Marketplace** — trusted realtors, lenders, builders, architects,
  designers, contractors, inspectors, surveyors, attorneys, insurance agents,
  trades, and home/furniture/flooring/tile/lighting/cabinet/countertop stores:
  view profiles, save favorites, request appointments, secure messages, upload
  files, share project details. *(Needs vendor data — gated seam.)*
- **Magical AI concierge** — "You need homeowners insurance before closing." ·
  "You haven't selected your flooring yet." · "Your inspection is tomorrow." ·
  "Your lease expires in 60 days." · "Generate questions for tomorrow's
  walkthrough?" Timely, proactive housing guidance.

## Recommended build phasing

- **Phase A (now):** Housing Hub entry (9 pathways) + Build-a-Home first slice
  (live). Self-contained.
- **Phase B:** per-pathway dashboards + Home Document Vault + Selection Center +
  Builder Assistant + Moving Center. *(Needs auth + secure storage.)*
- **Phase C:** Search for Land / Buy / New Construction listing search, Housing
  Marketplace, Lease Generator, Home Design Studio AI previews, Magical AI
  concierge. *(Needs real-estate/vendor data, generation, and partners.)*

**Sequencing note:** Housing Hub is a large ecosystem; per the approved
Foundation Build Order (we are on Phase 1 — Family Vault), Phases B–C here follow
later. The Phase-A entry + Build-a-Home ship now without disturbing that order.

**Guardrail:** organizational & educational assistance only; directories,
listings, AI previews, and generated leases gated behind real data/partners and
appropriate disclaimers; documents stored per Family Vault permissions; property
availability and all costs confirmed with the customer's professionals.
