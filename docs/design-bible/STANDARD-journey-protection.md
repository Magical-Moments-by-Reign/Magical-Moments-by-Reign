# Master Design Bible — Optional Membership Add-On
## Journey Protection™ — Pause Your Journey

**Status:** Documented — **pending Founder approval AND a pricing-model
decision (see "Reconciliation needed" below).** Subordinate to Book I.
Trademark note: per the Founder's earlier directive, the ™ symbol should appear
in customer-facing UI only once the trademark process is complete; kept here in
the Founder's canonical text.

**Price:** $2.99 / month or $29.99 / year.

**Purpose:** let customers temporarily pause a paid membership during financial
hardship or other life events **without losing memories, website, or account.**

---

## Pause options

Pause the paid subscription for **1, 2, or 3 months** (30 / 60 / 90 days). No
documentation, no proof of hardship, no support call — the customer selects a
pause period and confirms.

## During the pause

Account is **not** deleted; memories protected; premium website preserved.
Premium features temporarily disabled — the customer **cannot**: upload photos/
videos/documents, edit or publish the website, create new Journeys, use premium
AI or planning tools, add storage, send premium invitations, manage premium
RSVP, or create new galleries.

The customer **may**: log in, view existing content, download their own files,
use all Free Forever features, and resume anytime. Existing memories remain
viewable per their privacy settings.

## Resuming

Resume anytime — everything returns exactly where they left it. No files lost,
no rebuilding.

## Billing

- **Monthly members:** the pause period (1–3 mo) is added before the next
  billing cycle.
- **Annual members:** the pause period extends the annual renewal date by the
  same amount. (e.g. expires Jan 1, paused Oct–Dec → new expiration Apr 1.)
- Customers never lose time they paid for.

## Usage limit

One pause per active billing period. Monthly: one per rolling 12 months.
Annual: one per annual term. **Lifetime members:** not needed (no recurring
payments).

---

## ⚠ Reconciliation needed (Founder decision)

Journey Protection assumes **recurring monthly/annual memberships** and a
**"Free Forever" tier.** The currently approved pricing (Book: pricing) is
**one-time, term-based**: Silver (1 yr $249), Gold (5 yr $799), Diamond (10 yr
$1,499), Lifetime ($2,499 one-time), Custom Concierge ($5,000). There is no
monthly/annual subscription or Free Forever tier today.

Per Governance ("if something conflicts with the Bible, stop and request
clarification"), this add-on is **not built** until the Founder chooses one of:

1. **Introduce recurring plans + a Free Forever tier** (a pricing-model change —
   Journey Protection then applies to those).
2. **Adapt Journey Protection to the term-based model** — e.g. a "Journey Pause"
   that freezes an active term's countdown for 1–3 months and adds that time to
   the expiration date (no new recurring charge; offered free or as a one-time
   add-on the Founder prices).
3. **Defer** until the membership model is finalized.

No pricing, plan, or billing change will be made without explicit Founder
approval of one of the above.
