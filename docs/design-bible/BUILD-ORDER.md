# Foundation Build Order v1.0 — APPROVED

**Approved by Founder:** Tabitha Coetha Turner, Founder & Visionary.
Subordinate to Book I and the Governance standard. No production release,
database redesign, feature, architecture, UI/UX, roadmap, pricing, or AI
workflow change is final without Founder review and approval.

> We are not building a collection of websites. We are building a **Life
> Operating System.** Every Journey connects to every other Journey. Build one
> foundation at a time — quality over speed, scalability over shortcuts, legacy
> over trends.

## Order

1. **The Family Vault (first foundation)** — profiles (parent/child), emergency
   contacts, medical, insurance, document library, school/vehicle/home/pet
   centers, estate planning/wills, trusted contacts, permissions, family
   dashboard. **Every Journey must attach to the Family Vault — nothing exists
   independently.** *(In progress.)*
2. **Global Message Center** — family/couple/vendor/planner/AI messaging, email
   bridge, voice messages, voice-to-text, attachments, shared notes. (Video
   later.)
3. **The Journey Engine** — ONE reusable engine every Journey inherits:
   timeline, gallery, video gallery, story, voice/AI notes, calendar,
   checklists, budget, registry, cash gifts, guests, RSVP, messaging,
   notifications, vendor directory, search, permissions, memory-book export.
   Build once, reuse forever.
4. **The Life Engine** — think in life chapters; connect every Journey into one
   family story (Proposal → Wedding → … → Legacy).
5. **Baby Journey** (inherits the Journey Engine + pregnancy specifics).
6. **Wedding Journey** (most comprehensive planning experience).
7. **New Home Journey** (buying → building → closing → moving → maintenance).
8. **Marketplace** (trusted professionals & businesses).
9. **Magical AI** (the family concierge — plan, organize, remember, prepare,
   celebrate, protect, preserve).

## Global principles

Every feature answers: *"How does this make life easier?"* Every Journey
connects to every other. Every reminder reduces stress. Every upload becomes
part of the family's legacy. The platform works quietly in the background so
families can focus on living.
