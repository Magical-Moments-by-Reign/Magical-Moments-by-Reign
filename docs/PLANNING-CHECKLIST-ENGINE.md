## 1. Planning Purpose

Turn education and goals into a **clear, personalized path forward.** The engine translates
goal, life stage, deadlines, documents, family roles, and professional involvement into an
organized plan so a member always knows: the next step · what must happen first · what can
happen later · who is responsible · what documents are needed · which deadlines matter ·
where professional help is required · what's complete · what's waiting · what needs
attention — and it celebrates progress. It reduces stress **without becoming rigid or
overwhelming.**

---

## 2. Planning Philosophy

Plans feel like a **calm concierge has organized everything** — personal, flexible, clear,
supportive, adaptable, permission-aware, age-appropriate, Estate-aware, and honest about
uncertainty. Never a corporate board. The member stays in control; the engine reduces load.

---

## 3. Plan Creation

A plan may be created from: a member-selected goal · a concierge conversation · Estate
onboarding · learning-path completion · an upcoming milestone · a family invitation · a
professional recommendation · an admin-approved template · existing journey data · an
imported checklist · a member-created custom plan.

**Before creating a plan, the engine may ask** (only what it needs): what are you trying to
accomplish? · when would you like to finish? · what stage are you in? · who's involved? ·
what documents do you already have? · are you working with a professional? · what's your
budget? · what jurisdiction applies? · what concerns you most? · how much guidance would you
like?

**Rule:** the engine **never generates a plan on assumptions it can't verify.** Unknowns
are asked or left honestly open — never invented.

## 5. Checklist Item Structure

Each item may carry: title · plain-language explanation · why it matters · responsible
person · due date · suggested completion date · dependency · priority · status · estimated
effort · required documents · related learning · related calculator · related professional ·
related milestone · notes · attachments · reminder preference · permission visibility ·
completion evidence · completion confirmation · concierge guidance.

**Design rule:** an item is understandable **without opening multiple screens** — the title
+ one-line "why" + the next action are enough at a glance; detail expands on demand.

## 6. Task States

| State | Interface | Reminders | Concierge |
|-------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Not Started | quiet, ready-to-begin | none | "here when you want to start" |
| Ready | highlighted as next | gentle | "this is your next step" |
| In Progress | active | on due approach | offers help |
| Waiting | shown as paused-by-design | status-based | sets expectations |
| Waiting on Family | names who | to that person | can prepare a nudge (confirm) |
| Waiting on Professional | names the pro/step | on response window | can prepare a follow-up |
| Needs Information | asks for the missing input | prompt | asks the question |
| Needs Document | links the vault | on missing | "let's add this document" |
| Needs Approval | flags approver | to approver | routes approval |
| Needs Attention | calmly flagged | prompt (not nagging) | explains what & why |
| Blocked | shows blocker + what's first | none until unblocked | explains the dependency |
| Overdue | gentle, not alarming | escalates per prefs | "no worries — let's reschedule?" |
| Completed | checked, celebrated if a milestone | off | may celebrate |
| Skipped | recorded, reversible | off | notes it's skipped |
| Not Applicable | hidden/greyed | off | explains if asked |
| Archived | read-only | off | available on request |

Rule: a member only ever sees the **true** state; "Needs Attention" and "Overdue" stay
supportive, never shaming.

## 7. Dependencies & Order

Tasks can depend on one another — e.g. **preapproval before an offer**; **venue before final
invitations**; **college acceptance before housing registration**; **death certificate
before certain estate tasks.**

The engine: explains **why** a task is blocked · shows what must happen first · avoids
creating impossible deadlines · **recalculates future tasks when dates change** · **asks
before changing member-created dates** · preserves completed work · **never silently
deletes dependent tasks.** When a dependency shifts, downstream suggested dates move and the
member is shown the change, not surprised by it.

---

## 8. Deadlines & Timelines

Supports: fixed deadlines · estimated deadlines · recommended timelines · recurring
deadlines · annual renewals · countdown milestones · flexible target dates ·
professional-provided dates · official-agency dates · state-specific deadlines ·
family-selected dates.

**Every deadline shows its source:** Official · Professional-provided · Member-entered ·
Template recommendation · Concierge suggestion · Estimated.

**Rule:** **estimated dates are never presented as official deadlines.** Source and
confidence are always visible; official/agency dates carry their source link (from the
Education Engine's provenance).

---

## 9. Personalization

Plans adapt to: Estate · goal · current stage · age · role · family structure ·
jurisdiction · budget · timeline · experience level · documents already available ·
professionals already selected · accessibility needs · communication preferences · risk
level · member-selected guidance level.

Plans become **more relevant over time without taking control from the member** — the engine
suggests, reorders, and surfaces the next step; the member always decides. Guidance level
("just the essentials" vs. "walk me through everything") scales the detail shown.

## 10. Family & Collaboration

Members may assign/share tasks with: spouse · partner · parent · guardian · child · teen ·
grandparent · family member · caregiver · mentor · vendor · licensed professional · trusted
guest.

**Permissions determine:** who may view · edit · complete · reassign · upload documents ·
receive reminders · see financial details · see private notes · invite others. (Built on the
existing Family / Invitation / CollaboratorPermission systems.)

**Hard rule:** a shared task **never exposes private Estate information** — sharing a task
shares only that task's permitted fields, never the surrounding private context, financials,
or notes.

---

## 11. Child & Teen Planning

Age-appropriate, guardian-controlled:
- **Children:** chores · school reminders · savings goals · birthday planning · packing
  lists · family responsibilities · simple milestone tasks.
- **Teens:** scholarship tasks · college applications · career exploration · first-job prep ·
  bank-account appointments · driver milestones · volunteer hours · graduation requirements ·
  family-event responsibilities.

Parents/guardians retain permission control. The engine **supports independence without
bypassing safeguards** (guardian visibility and approval per the Concierge Architecture §5–6).

---

## 12. Professional Involvement

Professionals may contribute **without taking over** the member's plan. Eventually they may:
suggest a task · supply a deadline · request a document · mark a professional-side
requirement complete · add preparation instructions · confirm an appointment · provide
licensed guidance · upload approved information.

The member (or authorized owner) controls whether a professional is added. Professional
actions are: **permission-limited · audit-logged · clearly attributed · revocable · separate
from private family notes.** (Reuses the Vendor booking/compliance systems.)

---

## 13. Concierge & Planning

The concierge may: help create a plan · explain the next task · reorder suggested tasks ·
identify missing information · create reminders · summarize progress · prepare family updates
· suggest related education · link required documents · prepare questions for professionals ·
celebrate completed milestones · identify risks/blockers · ask whether dates should be
adjusted.

**It must request confirmation before:** assigning tasks to another person · sending reminders
to family · sharing documents · contacting professionals · cancelling appointments · changing
official deadlines · removing completed work · creating purchases/bookings · submitting
applications. (Consistent with Concierge Architecture §11.)

---

## 14. Reminders

Reminder kinds: due soon · overdue · waiting · appointment · renewal · milestone · missing
document · professional response · family response · payment · trial expiration · domain
renewal · celebration countdown.

**Member controls:** channel · frequency · quiet hours · escalation · morning/evening summary
· family visibility · snooze · pause · disable. Built on `Notification` /
`NotificationPreference`. **Reminders assist, never create anxiety** — consolidated,
respectful of quiet hours, always dismissible.

---

## 15. Plan Health

A **supportive** health view, not a stress score. Signals: On Track · Needs Attention ·
Waiting · Missing Information · Upcoming Deadline · Professional Support Needed · Paused ·
Completed.

**Rules:** avoid alarming red scoring unless there's genuine urgency; **do not invent a
success probability**; **do not claim a member will meet a goal based only on checklist
completion.** Health reflects real, honest state.

---

## 16. Templates

Reusable planning templates include: Estate · goal · stage · jurisdiction · age range · task
sequence · dependencies · suggested timing · required documents · related education ·
professional categories · milestones · safety notices · review date · version history.

Templates are **reviewed and versioned** (like Education content). **When a template changes,
existing member plans are never silently overwritten** — the member chooses to: keep current
plan · review updates · add newly recommended tasks · ignore changes.

---

## 17. Custom Tasks

Members can create their own tasks: custom title · notes · due date · assignee · privacy ·
reminder · attachments · recurrence · related Estate · related milestone.

**Rule:** custom tasks are **not treated as official guidance** unless the member explicitly
marks them so — a member's personal note never carries the weight of reviewed content.

---

## 18. Recurring & Long-Term Plans

Recurring planning for: home maintenance · vendor insurance renewal · school deadlines ·
birthdays · anniversaries · vehicle maintenance · health appointments · financial reviews ·
tax deadlines · domain renewal · estate-document reviews · subscription renewal · annual
family traditions.

**Rule:** recurring tasks **never duplicate endlessly.** Defined behavior for rollover (next
occurrence generated on completion/skip, not stacked), skip (missed once, next still comes),
completion (this occurrence done), and archival (series ended). (Reuses reminders + calendar.)

---

## 19. Pause, Change & Cancel (life changes)

When a wedding is postponed · a move cancelled · a home purchase falls through · a college
choice changes · a pregnancy plan changes · a vendor cancels · a family member leaves · a
member pauses an Estate · a goal is no longer relevant — the plan **preserves history,
completed work, documents, and memories. Do not erase the story.**

Allowed: pause · resume · change goal · change date · archive · duplicate · start a revised
plan. Completed work and captured memories always survive a change.

---

## 20. Milestones & Celebration

Task completion connects to celebration — e.g. preapproval completed · offer accepted · land
purchased · builder selected · scholarship submitted · college accepted · venue booked · baby
registry completed · funeral arrangements finalized · first savings goal reached · vendor
approved · home renovation complete.

Milestones feel **meaningful, not childish.** The concierge may say: *"You completed an
important step today. Your plan is moving forward beautifully."* Celebration **matches the
member's tone preference** (Concierge mode). Reaching a milestone prompts memory capture.

---

## 21. Plan Completion & Preservation

A plan may be: fully completed · completed with skipped items · completed by professional
confirmation · closed by member · converted into ongoing management · preserved as part of a
memory timeline · archived · continued into another Estate.

Completion leads naturally to: celebration · memory capture · document preservation · review ·
the next Life Estate · ongoing maintenance · family sharing. (Feeds the Framework's Memory
Preservation + cross-Estate continuity.)

## 22. Honest Empty & Failure States

Supportive, never fabricated:
- *No template:* "We can build this plan together from the beginning."
- *No deadline known:* "I don't have an official deadline for this step — want to add your own
  target date?"
- *No professional connected:* "This may need a licensed professional. I can help you prepare
  questions."
- *No documents:* "Your vault is ready when you have the first one."
- *Member doesn't know their goal:* gentle discovery, no pressure.
- *Member overwhelmed:* "Let's do just the next one thing together."
- *Task can't be completed / pro unavailable / integration disconnected:* say so plainly,
  offer the real alternative.
- *Missed deadline:* "No worries — let's reschedule," never shaming.
- *Conflicting information:* "These don't line up — which should I use?"

**Never** invent a deadline, a task, or a completion to fill a gap.

---

## 23. Safety & Trust

The Planning Engine must **never:** invent deadlines · guarantee outcomes · claim eligibility
· claim approval · fabricate professional availability · change high-stakes information
silently · share private tasks without permission · remove completed history without
confirmation · present a suggested template as official law · submit consequential forms
without approval.

Every regulated/high-risk plan preserves the **educational and professional boundaries** from
the Education & Concierge designs (educate → prepare → defer to a licensed professional).

## 26. Example Plans

**A) 🏡 Home — "buy my first home" (timeline plan).**
Discovery (goal, target date, budget, jurisdiction) → tasks: affordability → credit readiness
→ compare loan types (education + calculator) → save for down payment (financial goal) →
**preapproval** (Needs Document; blocks offer) → choose a realtor (professional-selection) →
tour & offer → inspection (waiting-on-professional) → closing (official dates, sourced) → keys
(milestone → celebrate → capture) → convert to home-maintenance (recurring). Dependencies
enforce preapproval-before-offer; deadlines show sources; nothing fabricated.

**B) 🎓 Education — "graduation & next step" (milestone plan).**
Requirements review (state-specific) → testing → applications (deadlines, sourced) →
financial aid → scholarships (recurring deadlines, proactive reminders) → decision →
enrollment (milestone → celebrate) → transition plan → continue into Career/Financial.

**C) 🕊 Legacy — "after a loss" (emergency/urgent plan).**
Minimal, gentle, prioritized: immediate needs → gather information → **death certificate**
(blocks dependent tasks) → funeral-home questions (prepared, not booked without confirm) →
notify chosen family (confirm before sending) → obituary worksheet (draft) → service details
→ preserve records & memories. Tone soft; nothing rushed or automated without consent.

All three use the **same** engine, structure, states, dependencies, and trust rules — only
configuration and content differ.
