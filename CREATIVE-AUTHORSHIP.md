## Human Review and Approval Workflow

Magical Moments by Reign is developed through a review-based workflow.

A typical development cycle may include:

1. The human product owner originates or requests a feature, design, change, correction, or new capability.
2. A development assistant implements or proposes the requested work.
3. The resulting implementation is placed into a development branch or pull request.
4. A deploy preview or other review environment may be created.
5. The human product owner reviews the result.
6. Additional revisions may be requested.
7. Work is revised until it meets the intended product direction.
8. Accepted work is merged or deployed.

This process may repeat many times for a single page or feature.

The existence of numerous revisions, redesigns, rejected implementations, replacement assets, renamed features, and amended workflows reflects continuing human creative judgment and control over the finished product.
